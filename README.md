# **Scenario-Based Report Development Using Prompt Engineering Techniques**

**Selected Use Case: Student Academic Performance Analysis**

## **2. Introduction**

Prompt engineering is the process of designing clear instructions for an AI system to generate accurate and relevant responses. Different prompting techniques help organize information, improve understanding, and obtain better results.

## **3. Selected Use Case – Student Academic Performance Analysis**

**Scenario:**

A college notices that some students are performing poorly in their examinations. The academic department collects attendance percentages and internal assessment marks to identify students who need academic support.

AI is used to analyze the data, identify performance levels, and suggest improvement strategies.

**Objectives:**

- Analyze student attendance and marks.
- Identify students with poor academic performance.
- Find possible reasons for low performance.
- Suggest suitable improvement methods.

### **Sample Student Data**

| Student | Attendance | Marks (50) |
| ------- | ---------- | ---------- |
| A       | 95%        | 45         |
| B       | 72%        | 28         |
| C       | 58%        | 19         |
| D       | 88%        | 40         |
| E       | 65%        | 23         |

## **4. Application of Prompt Engineering Techniques**

### **4.1 Straightforward Prompting**

**Definition:** Giving a direct instruction to AI to obtain the required response.

**Prompt:**

Analyze the academic performance of five students using their attendance and internal marks. Identify students with good and poor performance.

Student data: A (95%, 45), B (72%, 28), C (58%, 19), D (88%, 40), E (65%, 23).

**Expected Output:**

- Students A and D show good academic performance.
- Student B has average performance.
- Students C and E require additional academic support.

### **4.2 Tabular Format Prompting**

**Definition:** Instructing AI to organize information in rows and columns for easy comparison.

**Prompt:**

Analyze the academic performance of the five students and present the results in a table containing student name, attendance, marks, performance level, and improvement suggestions.

**Expected Output:**

| Student | Marks | Performance   | Suggestion           |
| ------- | ----- | ------------- | -------------------- |
| A       | 45    | Excellent     | Maintain consistency |
| B       | 28    | Average       | More practice        |
| C       | 19    | Poor          | Remedial classes     |
| D       | 40    | Good          | Regular revision     |
| E       | 23    | Below Average | Improve attendance   |

### **4.3 Preceding Question Prompting**

**Definition:** Asking preliminary questions before providing the main instruction to establish context and guide the AI response.

**Prompt:**

First, answer these questions:

1. Why is attendance important for students?
2. How do internal marks measure academic performance?
3. What are the common reasons for poor academic results?

Based on these answers, analyze the five students' attendance and marks. Identify students who need improvement and suggest suitable solutions.

**Expected Output:**

- Regular attendance helps students understand classroom concepts.
- Internal marks help evaluate subject knowledge.
- Poor preparation and irregular attendance may affect academic performance.
- Students C and E require additional support.
- Faculty mentoring, regular tests, and revision classes can improve results.

### **4.4 Missing Word Prompting**

**Definition:** Providing incomplete sentences or paragraphs and asking AI to fill in the missing words using contextual understanding.

**Prompt:**

Fill in the missing words using the following word bank.

**Word Bank:** Attendance, Performance, Assessment, Mentoring, Preparation.

1. Student academic \_\_\_\_\_\_ depends on several factors.
2. Regular \_\_\_\_\_\_ helps students participate in classroom activities.
3. Internal \_\_\_\_\_\_ marks evaluate students' knowledge.
4. Individual \_\_\_\_\_\_ provides guidance to weak students.
5. Proper examination \_\_\_\_\_\_ improves academic results.

**Expected Output:**

1. Performance
2. Attendance
3. Assessment
4. Mentoring
5. Preparation

## **5. Conclusion**

The four prompting techniques were successfully applied to the Student Academic Performance Analysis scenario. Each technique generated different types of responses based on the instructions provided. Straightforward prompting produced direct results, tabular prompting organized information, preceding question prompting established context, and missing word prompting completed incomplete sentences.

These techniques demonstrate how prompt engineering can help educational institutions analyze student performance and identify suitable improvement strategies.
